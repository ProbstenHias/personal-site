I'm Matthias. I work on internal developer platforms: the clusters, pipelines and golden paths that other engineers build on. Most of my day is Kubernetes and Go, plus the decisions, big and small, that keep a system boring in the best sense.

This site exists for two reasons. First, so that there is one place on the internet that says I exist. Second, as a notebook. When I learn something the hard way, I write it down here so that the next person (often me) learns it the easy way.

I prefer short answers first and details after. Every post starts with the conclusion. If the first line is all you need, you can stop there.
